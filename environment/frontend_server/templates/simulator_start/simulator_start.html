School Structure

Advocates for "One School for All" (Gemeinschaftsschule). They want to abolish the multi-tier school system to ensure students learn together for a longer period, preventing early social segregation.

Digitalization

Demands free digital devices (laptops/tablets) for every student. They heavily prioritize open-source software and strict data protection over commercial tech solutions in classrooms.

Equity & Inclusion

Total focus on social equality. Pushes for entirely free education, including free school meals, free public transport, and the complete abolition of hidden school fees. Strong advocates for inclusive learning environments.

Curriculum & Workforce
