### Terminology Footnotes

<a id="fn1"></a>
1. **Gemeinschaftsschule:** A comprehensive school model in Germany where children of all ability levels learn together from primary grades up through grade 10 or 13, avoiding early track separation.  
<a id="fn2"></a> 
2. **Hort:** An after-school care program, usually located at or near primary schools, providing supervision, homework help, and leisure activities.  
<a id="fn3"></a>
3. **Förderschule:** A specialized school designated specifically for children with specific physical, mental, emotional, or learning disabilities.  
<a id="fn4"></a>
4. **Regelschule:** A secondary school track in Thuringia that combines vocational and intermediate general education (covering grades 5–9 or 5–10).  
<a id="fn5"></a>
5. **Gymnasium:** The highest academic track of German secondary education, preparing students directly for university-level studies.  
<a id="fn6"></a>
6. **BLF (Besondere Leistungsfeststellung):** An exam taken at the end of 10th grade in a Gymnasium in Thuringia to certify an intermediate qualification level.  
<a id="fn7"></a>
7. **Realschulabschluss:** The intermediate school leaving certificate obtained after successful completion of the 10th grade in secondary education.  
<a id="fn8"></a>
8. **Kopfnoten:** Traditional conduct grades reported on report cards assessing soft qualities like behavior, effort, participation, and orderliness rather than academic subject knowledge.  
<a id="fn9"></a>
9. **Abitur:** The final qualification exam taken at the end of secondary education in Germany, granting entry to universities.  
<a id="fn10"></a>
10. **MINT:** The German equivalent of STEM (Mathematics, Computer Science, Natural Sciences, Technology / Mathematik, Informatik, Naturwissenschaften, Technik).  
<a id="fn11"></a>
11. **Beutelsbacher Konsens:** A widely accepted set of guidelines for political and civic education in Germany, mandating political neutrality and prohibiting teacher indoctrination.  
<a id="fn12"></a>
12. **DaZ (Deutsch als Zweitsprache):** German as a Second Language—specialized instructional programs designed for non-native German-speaking students.  
<a id="fn13"></a>
13. **SAVE-Beauftragte:** School officers designated for Anti-discrimination, Diversity, and Empowerment (Schulbeauftragte für Antidiskriminierung, Vielfalt und Empowerment).
